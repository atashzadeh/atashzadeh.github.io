Today's 9/25, Dolly Parton day, and today is the day I leave the team I've been working on for 5 years.

It's truly bittersweet - I've grown to love this team, but it has taken a major toll of my mental health and I need to put myself first. 

To celebrate, I'm sharing my most recent illustration, one that I'm very proud of. 

![throne of deception](/assets/images/2026-09-25/throneofdeception.png)

I will always be true to myself and make weird, macabre horny artwork. I've always been fascinated by those [exaggerated codpieces](https://www.vintag.es/2025/08/king-henry-viii-armor.html) that Henry the VIII's puny ego was obsessed with, and I'm of course also a fan of extremely thinly veiled metaphors. This idea knocked around my head for a little while and then spilled out all at once.
