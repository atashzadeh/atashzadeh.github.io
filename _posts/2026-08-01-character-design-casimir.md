Another character design up! This is my novel's antagonist, Casimir Lacrimiscu, vampire prince. 

![casimir lacramiscu](/assets/images/2026-08-01/casimir.png)

The rococo influence is much stronger here versus [Sonatine's initial design](character-design-sonatine-addendum). I wanted a big red silk cloak, lots of drama, a la [Alexander McQueen savage beauty couture](https://media.vogue.co.uk/photos/5d547cec5da545000800e782/master/w_2048%2Cc_limit/savage-beauty-64-vogue-12mar15-pr_b.jpg). The man is a fop, but he's an evil fop. 

This is the second round of design, below is the first phase.

![casimir lacramiscu 2](/assets/images/2026-08-01/casimir_old.png)

There is some more Transylvanian folk influence there in the sash itself, but I decided to keep this minimal in the blouse / vest combo. He's royalty! He doesn't want to feel like a peasant. 

I've also been playing with the idea of him wearing a mask in lieu of a crown. His nickname is The White Lion, so a lion's mask would be most appropriate.
